\chapter{Discussion}\label{chapter:Discussion}

This chapter interprets the empirical findings reported in Chapter~\ref{chapter:Results}, and discusses the five research questions stated in Table~\ref{tab:research-questions}. It is an interpretive chapter: no new measurements are reported, and no new experiments are run. Every claim is either based on the results of Chapter~\ref{chapter:Results}, or is a caveat that is already declared as a limitation in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-limitations}. Where the results are mixed, especially in the cost-quality comparison of RQ3, the interpretation is stated directly: the negative results are not buried.

This chapter follows the order of the system: Section~\ref{section:disc-summary-of-findings} summarizes the five findings that are in the remainder of this chapter. Sections~\ref{section:disc-doc-extraction-and-provenance} and~\ref{section:disc-knowledge-extraction-quality-and-stability} interpret the document-extraction and provenance results (RQ1, RQ2), the quality of the knowledge-extraction output (RQ2), and its stability under a random held-out set (RQ5). Section~\ref{section:disc-agreement-based-cascading} interprets the results of the cascade's cost-quality comparison (RQ3), and discusses the value of agreement-based cascading beyond what the defined silver metric can measure. Sections~\ref{section:disc-grounding-polarity-conflicts-and-safety-gates} and~\ref{section:disc-nli-as-shared-backbone} interpret the grounding filter, the safety gates, and how NLI is used as a shared backbone (RQ4). Section~\ref{section:disc-threats-to-validity} discusses the threats that bound the interpretation of every reported result; and finally, Section~\ref{section:disc-chapter-summary} summarizes the chapter.
