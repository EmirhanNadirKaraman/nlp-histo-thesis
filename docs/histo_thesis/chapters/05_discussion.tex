\chapter{Discussion}\label{chapter:Discussion}

This chapter interprets the empirical findings reported in Chapter~\ref{chapter:Results} and discusses the five research questions stated in Table~\ref{tab:research-questions}. No new measurements are reported, and no new experiments are run in this chapter. Every claim is either grounded in the results of Chapter~\ref{chapter:Results} or flagged as a limitation in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-limitations}. Where the results are mixed, especially in the cost-quality comparison of RQ3, the interpretation is stated directly: the negative findings are not buried.

% Section~\ref{section:disc-summary-of-findings} previews the five findings developed in the rest of the chapter. 

The chapter follows the order of the system. Section~\ref{section:disc-doc-extraction-and-provenance} covers the document-extraction and provenance results (RQ1, RQ2); Section~\ref{section:disc-knowledge-extraction-quality-and-stability} interprets the knowledge-extraction quality (RQ2) and its stability under a random held-out set (RQ5); Section~\ref{section:disc-agreement-based-cascading} assesses the results of the cascade's cost-quality comparison (RQ3) and discusses the value of agreement-based cascading beyond what the silver standard can measure. Sections~\ref{section:disc-grounding-polarity-conflicts-and-safety-gates} and~\ref{section:disc-nli-as-shared-backbone} interpret the grounding filter and relation classification (RQ4), the safety gates, and the shared NLI backbone. Section~\ref{section:disc-threats-to-validity} discusses the threats that bound the interpretation of every reported result.
