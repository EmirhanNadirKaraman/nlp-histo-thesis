\chapter{Methodology}\label{chapter:Methodology}

This chapter describes how the system processes a corpus of histopathology papers and converts them into a set of structured biomedical claims, each traceable to the paper and paragraph from which it was derived. The system consists of two pipelines, connected by a relational database: a document-extraction pipeline, which parses each PDF into text elements, figures, tables, and the cross-references between them, while preserving provenance; and a knowledge-extraction pipeline, which decomposes the extracted text into atomic findings, and refines them through grounding, normalization, grouping, canonicalization, relation classification, and scoring stages. 

At the core of the knowledge-extraction pipeline is the MAP stage, made cost-aware by agreement-based cascading: independent voters at a cheap tier are run first, and the chunk is escalated to a more capable (but expensive) tier only when they disagree (§\ref{section:Agreement_Based_Cascading}). The system is designed to surface traceable rule candidates for expert review; it does not aim to produce clinical rules autonomously.

This chapter follows the flow of data throughout the system. Section~\ref{section:system_overview} presents the end-to-end architecture and the principles guiding its design and implementation: reproducibility, provenance, and modularity. Section~\ref{section:Corpus} describes the corpus drawn from PubMed Central Open Access Subset, and the datasets used for calibration and evaluation of the pipeline. Then, Sections~\ref{section:Document_Extraction} and~\ref{section:Knowledge_Extraction} explain the document- and knowledge-extraction pipelines. Later, in Section~\ref{section:Agreement_Based_Cascading}, the agreement-based cascading mechanism that is used at the first stage of the knowledge-extraction pipeline is described in detail. Finally, Section~\ref{section:Evaluation_Methodology} defines how the five research questions of Table~\ref{tab:research-questions} were measured: document-extraction faithfulness (RQ1), knowledge-extraction accuracy and provenance carry-rate (RQ2), cascade cost-quality trade-off (RQ3), the behavior of the grounding and relation classification gates (RQ4), and the generalization of the calibrated configuration to a held-out set of papers (RQ5).
