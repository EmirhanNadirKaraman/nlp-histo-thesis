\subsection{Agreement-based cascading evaluation}\label{subsec:eval-abc}

The agreement-based cascading evaluation compares the calibrated cascading system to single-model baselines on the calibration cluster in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-knowledge-extraction} (RQ2/RQ3), and its generalization to the disjoint held-out set is assessed separately as RQ5 in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-heldout-generalization}.

The cascade configuration is selected using the offline replay procedure described in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-reprod-offline-replay}, in a screen-then-refine sequence: a screen over every combination of embedder (\texttt{gemini}, \texttt{openai}), agreement scorer (\texttt{embedding}, \texttt{hybrid}), and alignment strategy (\texttt{greedy}, Hungarian, \texttt{soft\_max}); a weight refinement of surviving structures (the \texttt{hybrid} weights and the \texttt{soft\_max} parameters); a single-voter-drop sweep (leave-one-voter-out), in which each voter is removed one after the other to select the strongest voter subset; and a later experimental pass over the agreement threshold $\theta \in \{0.3, 0.4, \dots, 0.9\}$ and the rejection threshold $\theta_{\mathrm{reject}} \in \{0.0, 0.1, 0.2\}$. The safety gate policies of Subsection~\ref{subsec:abc-safety-gates} are then evaluated as a separate $2\times2$ ablation (single-voter policy $\times$ polarity-conflict forcing) at the pinned configuration, after the joint sweep on the other variables is complete. The best configuration values found on the calibration set are reported as empirical results in Section~\ref{section:results-abc-calibration}. 

All comparisons use identical matcher settings on the calibration-cluster's silver findings, so that the comparisons differ only in the generated pipeline outputs. Strict-F1 score of each system, as well as cost (per-chunk escalation rate and incurred cost), is reported in Section~\ref{section:results-cost-quality-comparison}.

To determine whether the strict-F1 differences between the cascade and the single-model baselines are statistically significant, the thesis uses a paper-level clustered paired bootstrap: in each of $B = 10\,000$ resamples, papers are resampled with replacement, and the strict-F1 gap is recomputed. Papers are selected as the sampling unit because findings from the same paper are not independent. The resulting confidence intervals are reported in Section~\ref{section:results-cost-quality-comparison}.
