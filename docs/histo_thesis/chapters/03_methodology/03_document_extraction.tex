\section{Document Extraction Pipeline}\label{section:Document_Extraction}

\makeatletter
\def\input@path{{chapters/03_methodology/03_document_extraction}}
\makeatother

This section describes the document-extraction pipeline, which transforms the selected PMC Open Access histopathology papers into persistent representations of document text, sections, figures, tables, captions, and related evidence units for the downstream knowledge extraction of Section~\ref{section:Knowledge_Extraction}. The preceding corpus-filtering steps, namely histopathology keyword selection, the single-PDF self-containedness requirement, and the page-count cap, are described in Section~\ref{section:Corpus} and are not repeated here.

The focus of this section is to explain the design of the document-extraction pipeline, the role of PDF and XML representations, and the structure of the extracted outputs. The performance of the pipeline on the rubric-based evaluation on a subset of papers is reported separately in Chapter~\ref{chapter:Results}, and discussed in Chapter~\ref{chapter:Discussion}.
