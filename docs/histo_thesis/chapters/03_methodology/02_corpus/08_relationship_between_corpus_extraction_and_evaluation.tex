\subsection{Relationship between corpus, document extraction, calibration, and evaluation}\label{subsec:relationship-of-corpus-with-datasets}

The paper-based datasets drawn from the 977-paper ingested corpus (§\ref{subsec:ingestion-counts}) are: a document-extraction rubric set; a calibration cluster selected by integer linear programming based on mutual entity relatedness, described in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-ilp}; and a held-out generalization set. The 300 claim-pair dataset used to evaluate the relation classifier is constructed independently of the corpus. Table~\ref{tab:dataset-purpose-mapping} summarizes how each dataset is sampled or generated, and where it is used.

The document-extraction and knowledge-extraction datasets do not overlap at the paper level: none of the 27 rubric PDFs are included in the 15-paper calibration cluster; similarly, the held-out evaluation set is completely disjoint from the other sets. This separation keeps the calibration, document-extraction evaluation, and knowledge-extraction evaluations independent and prevents information leakage between the document-extraction and knowledge-extraction pipelines.
