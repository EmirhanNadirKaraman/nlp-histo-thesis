\subsection{Relationship between corpus, figure-table extraction, calibration and evaluation}\label{subsec:relationship-of-corpus-with-datasets}

The 977-paper ingested corpus supports the entire thesis; from the ingested corpus, a 27-PDF subset was selected for the evaluation of figure and table extraction, as well as a 15-paper calibration cluster. The 15-paper calibration cluster was generated by an ILP-algorithm, scoring each paper on their relatedness to each other and picking 15 papers that are the most related (the details of the algorithm are explained in detail in Subsection~\ref{subsec:eval-ilp}. From this 15-paper subset, 273 knowledge-extraction source cases were generated. 

The knowledge-extraction pipeline is evaluated separately on a 300-claim pair dataset, constructed independently of the corpus. Table~\ref{tab:dataset-purpose-mapping} summarizes how each dataset is sampled or generated, and how it is used in the thesis.

The document and knowledge extraction datasets do not overlap at the paper level: the 27-PDF figure-table extraction evaluation PDFs do not share any papers with the 15-paper calibration cluster.
