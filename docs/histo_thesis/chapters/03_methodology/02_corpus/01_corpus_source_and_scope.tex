\subsection{Corpus source and scope}\label{subsec:corpus-source-and-scope}

The corpus used in this thesis is drawn from the Open Access subset of PubMed Central, maintained by the U.S. National Library of Medicine~\parencite{nlm_pmc_open_access}. PMC OA is suitable for this thesis for three reasons: first, every article in this subset is made available under a Creative Commons or similar license, allowing data processing, redistribution, and analysis at scale. Second, each article is available as a PDF and in JATS-XML format; although this thesis does not use XML files, they can serve as a supplementary tool in the document extraction pipeline. Third, PMC OA offers a large body of biomedical papers, from which a histopathology-focused subset of sufficient size could be selected. 

Candidate papers were selected from the PMC OA file index by matching each paper's title and journal against two case-insensitive keyword sets. The inclusion set targets terms specific to histopathology: \emph{histopathology}, \emph{histology}, \emph{pathology}, \emph{whole slide image} (\emph{WSI}), \emph{digital pathology}, \emph{hematoxylin}, \emph{eosin} (\emph{H\&E}), \emph{immunohistochemistry} (\emph{IHC}), \emph{biopsy}, \emph{resection}, \emph{stained section}; it also includes the subfields of histopathology: \emph{dermatopathology}, \emph{neuropathology}, \emph{cytopathology}, and \emph{surgical pathology}. The exclusion set removes papers whose main focus is not tissue-level pathology, but rather radiological imaging (\emph{MRI}, \emph{CT}, \emph{ultrasound}, \emph{X-ray}), endoscopic procedures, and molecular assays (\emph{Western blot}, \emph{flow cytometry}, \emph{PCR}, \emph{RNA-seq}). It also excludes non-human studies, removing each article that contains keywords such as \emph{veterinary}, \emph{animal model}, \emph{mouse}, \emph{rat}, and \emph{murine}. 

An article is kept only if it contains at least one keyword from the inclusion set and no keywords from the exclusion set. The surviving PMC IDs are then downloaded through the PMC OA service. Since keyword filtering is based on title and journal rather than full text, and since the inclusion keywords are English, the resulting corpus is effectively English-language and focused on human tissue, even though no explicit language filter is applied.

The target papers focus on tissue-level pathology findings, biomarker expressions, morphological assessments, staging, diagnostic interpretations, prognostic associations, and related biomedical claims. The scope of selected papers is deliberately narrow, chosen to provide a focused dataset for evaluating the system on histopathology-oriented information extraction. Its limitations, including the histopathology-only focus and English-only coverage, are stated in Subsection~\ref{subsec:corpus-limitations-of-data}.
