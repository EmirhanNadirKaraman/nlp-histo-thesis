\appendix
\chapter{Use of Artificial Intelligence}
\label{appendix:ai-usage}

I used Claude during the preparation of this thesis to assist with programming, testing, refactoring, and debugging. Additionally, AI suggestions were used for parts of the implementation that involved prompting large language models and required histopathology domain knowledge. All AI-assisted outputs were critically reviewed and validated. Grammarly was used for spell-checking and minor language polishing while writing the thesis.
