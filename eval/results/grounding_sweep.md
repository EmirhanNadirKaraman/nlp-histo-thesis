## Interpretation

- Higher `threshold` means stricter grounding (fewer findings kept).
- `kept` / `rejected` are simulated by the predicate `grounding_score >= threshold`.
- Records with missing `grounding_score` are excluded from the keep/reject decision and surfaced separately as `missing_score_count` / `missing_score_pct`.
- Percentages use **scored** records as denominator (`kept_pct_of_scored`, `rejected_pct_of_scored`). `missing_score_pct` uses **total** records as denominator.
